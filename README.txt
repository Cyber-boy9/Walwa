MOCHI'S WORLD - phase 1
1. Put your 5-6 songs in the songs/ folder (mp3 or m4a).
2. Open js/config.js and edit each song's title, artist and file name.
3. Open index.html (or upload the whole folder to Netlify/GitHub Pages).
Her choices (name, outfit, Mochi, song) are saved in her own browser, no backend.
